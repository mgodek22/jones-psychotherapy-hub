ADDING THE ORGANIZATION LOGOS  (optional, 10 minutes)
Resources page — Jones Psychotherapy
------------------------------------------------------------
The resource cards are "logo-ready." Each shows a styled badge by
default. Drop an official logo image into THIS folder with the exact
filename below and it AUTOMATICALLY replaces the badge on that card.
No file? The badge shows instead — nothing breaks. Emily's own logo
(jones.png) is already here.

WHY THESE AREN'T INCLUDED
Each logo is the trademark of that organization. We don't have the
right to redistribute their image files, so they can't ship inside
this package. As the site owner linking to these groups as resources,
you can add their official logos yourself — most publish a press or
brand kit, or will email one if you ask.

SAVE EACH AS (PNG with a transparent or white background, ~square,
about 200x200px is plenty):

  samhsa.png            samhsa.gov  (see their brand/logo guidance)
  nami.png              nami.org  (About > Newsroom / brand assets)
  emdria.png            emdria.org
  ifs.png               ifs-institute.com
  agpa.png              agpa.org
  adaa.png              adaa.org
  psi.png               postpartum.net
  mha.png               mhanational.org
  apa.png               apa.org
  psychology-today.png  Psychology Today gives therapists an official
                        "Verified by Psychology Today" badge inside
                        your member account — that is the correct,
                        licensed one to use here.
  jones.png             your own logo (already added)

TIP: crop tightly to the logo. Square images look best in the tiles.
